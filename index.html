How to play

How a run goes

You start with $1,000, enough for three Common blades. Everything after that is up to you.

  1. Buy off the conveyor

    A new blade rolls by every few seconds. Rarer ones cost more and earn far more. Common $300, Secret $4,000,000.

  2. Display it

    Put it on a podium in your shrine. It earns cash every second, even while you're offline, and the money piles up on the green pad in front.

  3. Steal or defend

    Walk into other shrines and carry their blades home. Raise your Barrier to seal yours for 60 seconds, or fight back in PvP.

  4. Rebirth

    Trade your cash and three blades for +10% income forever, a stat point, and one more podium on the 2nd floor.
